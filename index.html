01Now · Research

Building a digital twin of the forest.

Since Fall 2026 I've been an MS student in Biosystems Engineering at Auburn University, working as a Graduate Research Assistant under Dr. Harman Singh Sangha. My research uses LiDAR for tree inventory and to build a digital twin of the forest.

What you're looking at

  1. 01Scan

    A terrestrial laser scanner sweeps the stand and records every surface it hits as a 3D point.

  2. 02Segment

    Wood is separated from leaves, so each stem can be measured: height and diameter at breast height.

  3. 03Twin

    Every tree becomes a record in a digital model of the whole plot that can be measured again over time.
