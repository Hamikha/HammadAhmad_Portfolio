Projects

NeuroSpeakMate

This was my B.Sc. final-year project at UET Peshawar: a platform that lets non-verbal autistic individuals communicate by voice, text or gesture. I used deep learning and computer vision models for emotion detection, and an LLM to drive the interaction.

Card-AI

Card-AI reads an image of a Pokémon card and returns the card's details as structured data. I built it with computer vision and deep learning models and OCR.

Grace AI

I built Grace AI, a multi-tenant voice AI platform for automated scheduling, on FastAPI with the OpenAI Realtime API, ElevenLabs and Qdrant. It streams two-way audio over the web through Socket.IO and over the phone through Twilio, with custom voice activity detection and barge-in handling, and it has calendar integrations for Google, Outlook and Unipile.

graceai.com.au

Oper8

I built Oper8, a voice and email assistant for business automation. On the email side, an LLM classifies each incoming query and a tool-calling loop books appointments and replies through the Unipile API; the phone and web voice agents run in real time on Twilio, Socket.IO and ElevenLabs text-to-speech, with Qdrant as the vector store.

oper8.ae
